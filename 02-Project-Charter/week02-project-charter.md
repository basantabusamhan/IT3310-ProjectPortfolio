# Project Charter Draft: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System Replacement

**Consultant:** Basant Abusamhan

**Role:** Associate Consultant, NovaTech Consulting

**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)

**Date:** September 15, 2026

### Project Title

RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System Replacement

### Project Sponsor

RCHN Project Sponsor

### Business Need

RCHN needs to replace its aging patient intake and scheduling system across six clinics. The current system may not support the organization’s needs as effectively as a newer system could, and workflows may differ between clinics. NovaTech should confirm the current system’s limitations and understand how staff use it at each location. Replacing the system can provide a more consistent and reliable foundation for intake and scheduling while supporting the needs of RCHN and its clinics.

### Purpose and High-Level Scope

The purpose of the project is to replace RCHN’s aging patient intake and scheduling system across six clinics. The project will focus on understanding current clinic workflows, identifying system and process requirements, and supporting the transition to a system that better aligns with RCHN’s needs. Detailed implementation planning and requirements will be developed during later project phases.

### Success Criteria

The project will be successful when the new patient intake and scheduling solution meets the agreed requirements, supports the workflows of all six clinics, and provides a clear foundation for staff training and future process improvement. Success will also require agreement from key stakeholders that the solution supports the organization’s needs.

### Early Stakeholders

* **Project Sponsor:** Provides organizational priorities, project expectations, resources, and decisions needed to move the project forward.
* **Clinic Managers:** Provide information about current intake and scheduling workflows and differences between clinics.
* **Front Desk Staff:** Provide information about daily system use and current intake and scheduling challenges.
