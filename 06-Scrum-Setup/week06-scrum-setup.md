## Agile Delivery Fit

The scheduling workflow and intake forms could benefit from iterative delivery because the team can develop a basic version, get feedback from staff at the six clinics, and make improvements based on their needs. This would help the team identify problems early and adapt the system before it is fully developed. However, patient data security and access permissions require stronger advance planning and control to protect sensitive information and ensure that only authorized staff can access patient records.

---

## Scrum Team

**Product Owner:** RCHN business representative.

**Scrum Master:** NovaTech Scrum Master.

**Developers:** NovaTech's project team, including workflow and IT specialists.

### Rationale

The Product Owner makes sense for this role because they understand RCHN's needs and can prioritize the features that provide the most value. The Scrum Master helps the team follow Scrum, remove obstacles, and improve teamwork without taking over the team's responsibilities. The Developers are responsible for creating the scheduling workflow prototype, standardizing intake forms, and delivering work that meets the Sprint Goal. Their collaboration with clinic staff will help ensure the solution meets RCHN's needs.

## Sprint Planning Reflection

I selected these items because they support the Sprint Goal and address the highest-priority needs first. The scheduling workflow prototype will give clinic staff something concrete to review, while standardizing the intake forms will help all six clinics collect consistent information. The sprint communication update will also help keep stakeholders informed and encourage feedback. The total is 23 points, which stays within the 25-point planning limit and leaves some room for unexpected challenges. The team needs to learn how the scheduling workflow should function and what information the clinics need before moving on to additional features.

I intentionally deferred the clinic staff training outline because the workflow and intake forms should be reviewed first. Waiting will help the team create more accurate training materials after the process is better understood.

## Scrum Master Reflection

### Obstacle and Response

One impediment that could affect progress toward the Sprint Goal is difficulty getting feedback from staff at all six clinics. Each clinic may have different needs, which could make it harder for the team to agree on the scheduling workflow and intake forms. The Scrum Master could help by coordinating communication with clinic staff and arranging feedback sessions. They could also help the team discuss challenges and find solutions together. This supports the team without taking over their work or making decisions for them.

### Preparing for Week 7

This week's sprint planning will help me build the Product Backlog next week because I have identified the project's main priorities and what needs to be completed first. I can use the Sprint Goal to break larger tasks into smaller backlog items and organize them based on priority and business value. I can also use feedback from clinic staff to adjust priorities and make sure the team focuses on the most important needs first.
