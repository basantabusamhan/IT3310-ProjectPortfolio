Project Plan Foundation: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System

Consultant: Basant Abusamhan

Role: Associate Consultant, Project Manager, NovaTech Consulting

Engagement: Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)

Date: September 24, 2026

Scope
In Scope, Phase One
Patient intake workflow standardization across all six clinics
Appointment scheduling system replacement
Staff training for the new system at all six locations
Initial rollout support for 90 days after launch

Out of Scope, Phase One
Patient-facing online scheduling portal
Billing system integration with the new scheduling data
Executive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership
Mobile application for clinic staff
Scope Justification

The patient-facing online scheduling portal should be excluded from Phase One because the main focus of the project is replacing the scheduling system and standardizing intake workflows across the six clinics. Including the portal could increase the project scope and complexity and require additional resources and development time. It would be better to consider the portal in a future phase after the main system replacement is completed.

SMART Objectives
Objective 1

Standardize the patient intake workflow across all six RCHN clinics as part of the Phase One system replacement.

Connection to Product Vision: The Week 3 product vision focused on improving the intake and scheduling experience for clinic staff. Standardizing the intake workflow supports this vision by giving staff a more consistent process across all six locations.

Objective 2

Replace the current appointment scheduling system across all six RCHN clinics and provide staff training before the new system is used at each location.

Connection to Product Vision: The Week 3 product vision identified inconsistent scheduling workflows as an important problem. Replacing the current scheduling system and training staff supports the goal of creating a more consistent and efficient process.

Objective 3

Provide 90 days of initial post-launch rollout support to help staff address issues and adjust to the new intake and scheduling system.

Connection to Product Vision: The product vision focused on improving the experience for clinic staff and reducing workflow problems. Post-launch support helps staff adjust to the new system and provides an opportunity to address issues after implementation.

Planning Constraints
Constraint 1

The project must address the intake and scheduling workflows across all six RCHN clinics.

How it affects planning: This affects scope, resources, schedule, and risk because the team must plan for implementation and training across six different locations with potentially different workflow needs.

Constraint 2

Phase One is limited to the system replacement and related intake, training, and rollout activities identified in the approved project scope.

How it affects planning: This affects scope and resources because additional features such as a patient portal, billing integration, dashboard, or mobile application would increase the amount of work and complexity in the current project.

Constraint 3

Initial post-launch rollout support is limited to 90 days.

How it affects planning: This affects schedule and resources because the team must plan support activities within the 90-day period and determine how issues will be handled after the support period ends.

Assumptions
Assumption 1

We are assuming that the current intake and scheduling requirements can be identified and standardized across all six RCHN clinics.

If this is wrong, then the scope, requirements, schedule, and resources may need to change to account for major differences between the clinics.

Assumption 2

We are assuming that RCHN stakeholders will confirm the Phase One scope and proposed objectives before detailed scheduling and resource planning begins.

If this is wrong, then the project plan may need to be revised, including the scope, schedule, resources, and priorities.

Planning Reflection

One planning decision I made this week was to keep the patient-facing online scheduling portal out of Phase One. The scope and objectives work from this week, along with the earlier project charter, informed this decision because the main focus of the engagement is replacing the scheduling system and standardizing intake workflows across the six clinics. I also had to use my judgment to keep the first phase focused and avoid adding features that could increase the project complexity. Before this decision is treated as final, the RCHN sponsor should confirm that the portal can remain outside the Phase One scope.

Planning Foundation Synthesis

The planning decisions from this week connect the project scope, objectives, constraints, and assumptions. The main focus of Phase One is to standardize patient intake workflows, replace the scheduling system, train staff, and provide initial rollout support across the six RCHN clinics. The out-of-scope items are being excluded to keep the project focused and manageable. The constraints show the conditions that need to be considered when planning the work, while the assumptions identify information that still needs to be confirmed. These decisions provide a foundation for future scheduling, resource, risk, cost, and communication planning.
